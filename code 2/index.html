Welcome to New College Boat Club

Founded in 1840, New College Boat Club (NCBC) is one of Oxford’s most historic and competitive rowing clubs.
Based on the River Isis, we welcome rowers of all levels—from complete beginners to experienced athletes—
who want to be part of a dedicated and ambitious squad.

Early History & Successes

Founded in 1840, NCBC grew into a leading Oxford rowing club.Achieved Head of the River titles in 1896–1899 and 1911–1913.

Olympic & Henley achievements

Represented Great Britain at the 1912 Stockholm Olympics, winning silver. Henley Royal Regatta victories: Grand Challenge Cup (1897) Ladies’ Challenge Plate (1900, 1950) Stewards’ Challenge Cup (1912, 1913)

Recent Performance & Growth

Men’s 1st VIII 9th in Torpids 2025. Women's 1st VIII 10th in Torpids 2025 8 crews qualified for Summer Eights 2024, the most of any club.
